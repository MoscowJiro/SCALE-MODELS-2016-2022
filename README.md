# SCALE MODELS — 2016—2022

### Selected Works & Process Archive

*A visual archive of making, observation and material study.*

![Scale Models portfolio cover](assets/README/01_cover.jpg)

## Overview

I learned to observe before I learned to create.

Around the age of sixteen, scale modeling became my first systematic way of studying real objects.

Proportion, structure, material, color, weathering and historical references — each model required observation before reconstruction.

What began as a hobby gradually became a way of seeing.

**2016—2022 · 7 Years · 148 Photographs**
